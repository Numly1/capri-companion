# Capri Rich Presence animation

Rendered from the original Capri Desktop cutout and WebGL shader by mochiiwuff:
https://github.com/mochiiwuff/capri-desktop

Input: app/assets/capri/capri-full.png and app/src/renderer/js/sprite.js.
48 deterministic frames, 512 x 512, 12 fps, looping GIF, with a dark neon backdrop.
The movement uses the existing shader's arm and leg poses. The character art has
not been redrawn or replaced. A PNG of the opening frame is included as a fallback.

The original project's authorship and licensing notices apply; character imagery
belongs to its respective rights holders. This is a personal noncommercial fan companion.
